GLOBAL INSTRUCTION
Re-prompt the image generator and put the accepted result into the actual website asset files. QUALITY CHECK BEFORE INSERTING
Request the highest available NATIVE resolution, ideally at least 2048px tall for each isolated can. Inspect the returned file dimensions and the lettering at 100% before accepting it. Reject soft text, incorrect spelling, waxy metal, halos, blur, and clipped rims. Keep native-resolution PNG masters with transparency and lossless WebP exports. Never add artificial grain, sharpening halos, glow, or a blur filter to the actual can. Match the same silver shape, black vertical VKTRY mark, white flavor name, and colored band across all three flavors. Use the first accepted master as a visual reference for subsequent flavors.

If those pixels are insufficient for a larger retina or print application, re-prompt with a higher-native-resolution image tool or use a proper 3D product render with vector label artwork. Do not upscale and claim additional detail.
